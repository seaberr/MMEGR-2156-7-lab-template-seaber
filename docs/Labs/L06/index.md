# Lab 6 – Design Fits for an Artifact
### Instructions
In order to design a mated part, take measurements of an artifact's feature you wish to mate your design.
Parametrically design something small that snap fits or slides and sticks into one of the features of the artifact measured in class. (no key rings)
Use parameters in CAD
Use constraints in CAD
Place 2-3 student projects on one build plate.
Test your design.
Documentation:
### Parametrically design

(40%) Parametrically design something small that snap fits or slides into one of the features of the artifact measured in class. Document the thought and physical process and decisions from measuring the artifact to exporting the stl file. The documentation includes many pictures and images. Capture the design’s use of parameters and constraints.

What are the parameters used?
Why did you choose the specific parameters?
What values did you choose for the specific parameters?
Did the values change throughout the process? If so, why?
Take measurements of the needed feature of the artifact.
Create a hand sketch of the feature and include it in the portfolio.
Recreate the feature in CAD.
Take many pictures of the different stages of the CAD model.
Detail the decision making process and how you determined the engineered allowances of the interactive parts.
Take a picture of the overall design in CAD.
### Documentation

(20%) In your documentation record the 3D printing process from stl to finish product. Take a short video of your part as it is 3D printing. Make sure to test your print before class to ensure of the fit. Furthermore, some, not all questions, to answer are outlined below to guide your documentation.

What is the name machine you use?
What is the size of the print?
Outline reasons for the layout.
Outline reasons for build orientation.
Outline reasons for the size of supports used.
What was the wall thickness?
How many layers are used in the print?
What is the layer thickness?
What is the build volume of your print?
Outline slicer settings and reasons for the settings.
What did you use to remove the supports?
If the fit was not correct, how did you modify the design.
