### Lessons Learned
What I learned in this cad assignment along with last week is starting on creating a design in class isn't the best method for me because I could better use that time for working on other things or portfolio work. I learned this because This is a design class. I learn about what I am supposed to actually make in class one day. What do I do with that information, I think. If I immediately started and opened up Creo Parametric and started sketching out a part, it would most likely be terrible. Waiting a Day or to while brainstorming has been very beneficial for me because when I think I am ready to design my part, I know what I am doing because I already have designed a model in my head and it only takes me like an hour to actually finish it on the computer. I then can take it straight to the Printer lab after that. A lot of my lessons have to do with time management, but I also had another lesson related to design because the larger Part I 3d printed was supposed to hold the motor but it did not end up fitting because I did not account for the wiring and the trigger not being separable which led to the trigger for the motor not fitting through the motor holder.
