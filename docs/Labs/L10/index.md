# Lab 10 – Assignment-RC2 Sliding Fit Design and Verifaction
### Objective
Design and fabricate a pair of parts (a hole/slot feature and a matching shaft/insert feature) that demonstrate an RC 2 sliding fit on a SLA print as defined in the Machinery’s Handbook:

“RC 2 Sliding fits are intended for accurate location, but with greater maximum clearance than class RC 1. Parts made to this fit move and turn easily but are not intended to run freely, and in the larger sizes may seize with small temperature changes.”

You will:

Research the RC 2 fit specification from the Machinery’s Handbook.

Measure your printed features (hole/slot and shaft/feature) using calipers.

Show your printed part set in class to verify the fit and documentation.

Total volume of the two parts may not exceed 20mL including supports.
Application Examples of RC 2 fits

Accurate alignment components: It is a good choice for locating pins and parts that need to slide into place with minimal play.
Jigs and fixtures: In manufacturing, an RC2 fit is often used for locating pins or components on a jig or fixture to ensure repeatable positioning.
Sliding gears and pulleys: For gears and pulleys that need to be manually shifted or moved along a shaft, an RC2 fit provides enough clearance for movement without excessive wobble.
Low-speed sliding parts: An RC2 fit can be used for parts that move at slow speeds with light load, such as in control levers and similar mechanisms.
Telescoping assemblies: This fit is suitable for some precision sliding parts in telescoping assemblies where motion is intentional but not continuous.
Pin joints in mechanisms: For certain precision mechanisms, an RC2 fit can be used for a pin joint that needs to turn freely, such as a clevis and pin connection.
